## Project 1: Stellar Motion and Stellar Spectra Comparison

**Description:** 
- Analyze the data in the database of 7 kinds of stars by plotting the data in terms of wavelength spectrum and their intensity to determine the type of elements absorbed by hydrogen atom on the star HD 94028.
- Calculate the doppler effect by checking the difference between the most absorbed wavelength of the star and the standard wavelength of that particular element to determine whether the star is in redshift or blueshift relative to the Earth.
- Differentiate the stars which are in redshift and in blueshift and display them on the same graph.
