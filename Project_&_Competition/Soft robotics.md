### Soft Robotics Study

- Study on locomotion of a pneumatic-driven octopus device fabricated in silicon gel and investigated potential applications in robotics in space and engineering industry, such as tunneling, adaptation on diverse terrain. 
- Designed and modified mold CAD model for study- and testing-purpose
