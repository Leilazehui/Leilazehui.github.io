## Topics covered in this course
### Cold & flush water supply
- Introduction of cold and flush water supply systems in buildings and design calculations for cold and flush water system.

### Hot water supply
- Introduction of individual and centralized hot water supply.
### Above ground drainage
- Introduction of above ground drainage for soil and waste water.

### Below ground drainage
- Introduction of below-ground drainage which is a continuation of the drainage system for soil and wastewater
### Storm water supply
- Introduction of stormwater drainage which handles the drainage of rainwater

### Vertical transportation
- A brief introduction, some design considerations and the design of the escalators.

### Fuel gas supply
- Introduction of fuel gas supply in buildings.
